# A tip link (Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors)

Limpide asks for no money. About invites what helps it instead: sharing it, or a star on GitHub ([issue 172](../docs/issues/172-no-tip-link.md)).

## Why this is out of scope

Built in 1.19.0 ([issue 146](../docs/issues/146-a-tip-link-in-about.md)), then taken back by the owner on setting up the account, 2026-10-31: "Looks like complications for not much benefit. I do not think many people will send money." Receiving money means a payment account, identity and tax details, for a few coffees a year at most. It may come back, as GitHub Sponsors or the same link, if the synth world takes to Limpide (the spirit of v2: [issue 131](../docs/issues/131-v1.md), decision 12).
