# Arpeggiator

Limpide has no arpeggiator (held Notes played one after another at a rate, up, down or as played). Notes come from the player, the Demo, the Song and the Hold rhythm; a tempo may come ([issue 112](../docs/issues/112-tempo-midi-clock-and-an-arpeggiator.md)), for LFO sync, without an arpeggiator.

## Why this is out of scope

The owner, 2026-10-30: "This won't help people understand how synths work. Also the Webapp will be ported to a VST/AU plugin in future and that will just be a synth." The PRD lists it out of scope; [issue 112](../docs/issues/112-tempo-midi-clock-and-an-arpeggiator.md), decision 2.
