# Sections that switch off

No section of Limpide has an off switch or folds away: the Filter is always in the path, and an LFO is quiet when its Depth is 0 (its plot then dims).

## Why this is out of scope

The owner gave it up on 2026-10-30 ("Give up on 114 too"), to keep the project's scope from growing too big. With no effects or arpeggiator, the sections it was mostly for, little was left to switch off; [issue 114](../docs/issues/114-sections-that-switch-off.md).
