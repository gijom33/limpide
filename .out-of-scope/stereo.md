# Stereo

Limpide's sound is mono: one voice through the Filter and the Amp to the speakers, with no stereo spread, panning or width.

## Why this is out of scope

Limpide stays a mono voice, like the classic synths it teaches, and every Viewer reads one signal at each Tap. The owner kept the output mono when framing v1 ("OK with mono output", 2026-10-31, [issue 131](../docs/issues/131-v1.md), decision 5); width belongs to effects, which are out too ([effects](./effects.md)).
