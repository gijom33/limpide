# A double-click sending a fader to zero

A double-click on any knob or fader resets it to its default: the Osc 1 fader goes to 100 %, the other Mixer faders to 0.

## Why this is out of scope

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-29 for the Osc 1 fader, and withdrawn the same day, once told what a double-click already does: "that was a bad idea indeed." One gesture meaning one thing on every control is worth more than a quicker way to silence Osc 1, which its arrow keys (Home) or a drag already give.
