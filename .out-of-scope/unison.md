# Unison

Limpide has no unison (several detuned copies of each Oscillator on one Note, a "supersaw"). A thick sound comes from Osc 2 detuned against Osc 1, Chorus Drift's LFO on Detune, or several Voices playing a chord.

## Why this is out of scope

The Viewers follow one Note's Oscillators ([ADR-0007](../docs/adr/0007-optional-polyphony-viewers-follow-the-newest-note.md)), and unison would draw a Spectrum and an Ideal that no longer match what is heard. The PRD lists it out of scope; the owner kept it there in triage on 2026-10-30, [issue 117](../docs/issues/117-more-to-play-and-hear.md), decision 3.
