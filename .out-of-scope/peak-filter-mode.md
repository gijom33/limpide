# A peak Filter mode

The Filter's modes are low-pass, high-pass, band-pass and notch; there is no peak (a boost around the Cutoff with nothing cut).

## Why this is out of scope

A peak boosts a band without removing anything, more an equaliser than a synth filter, and the notch already teaches a Filter that shapes a band. Turned down by the owner in triage on 2026-10-30, [issue 117](../docs/issues/117-more-to-play-and-hear.md), decision 5.
