# Averaging the Scope's cycles

The Scopes do not average several aligned cycles to steady a trace ([issue 180](../docs/issues/180-steadier-scopes.md), decision 6).

## Why this is out of scope

Measured 2026-11-02 ([research](../docs/research/180-steadier-scopes.md)): averaging lies where it looks steadiest. A Perfect Fifth stands still as a wave 82 % off the real one, and Noise is erased. A Scope must show the sound as it is; the steadiness comes instead from locking to the pitch played and matching the last frame, with a Glow for those who like a softer trace. Decided by the owner, 2026-11-02, taking the recommendation.
