# 122: Match the sound

**What to build:** From Claude's brainstorm, accepted by the owner in triage ([118](./118-new-ways-to-learn.md), decision 1, 2026-10-30): Limpide plays a hidden Patch, and the player turns knobs to match it, the Spectrum showing how close; ear training as a game.

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** needs-triage · enhancement. To grill with the owner before anything is built.

## What a grilling would settle

- Where the challenges come from: a Preset with a few knobs moved from Init, generated, or a fixed course from easy (one knob) to hard.
- Which knobs are in play in each round, and how the rest are locked or hidden.
- How close is measured (the Spectrum's distance, per knob, or both) and how it is shown: a score, a meter, the target's Spectrum drawn as a ghost.
- Hearing the target again, switching between it and one's own sound, and hints.
- Where it opens from (beside Tutorial and Demo?), and how it ends and is remembered.
- Phones and the C16.
