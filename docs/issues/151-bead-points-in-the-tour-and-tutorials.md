# 151: Bead points the way in the Tour and the Tutorials

**What to build:** The owner's idea (inbox, 2026-10-31): "Have Bead help focus on right element during the tour and tutorials." Today a Tutorial Step rings its control, with its target above it ("→ 800 Hz"), and marks its section in a phone's strip; the Tour's cards do the same for what they show. Bead ([115](./115-an-easter-egg.md)) would go to that control and draw the eye to it, so a newcomer finds the right knob at once.

**Blocked by:** the owner's answers below; [150](./150-bead-shown-by-default.md) (Bead shown by default), since otherwise most people taking the Tour have not met Bead

**Status:** needs-triage · enhancement. Claude's call, open to the owner.

## Questions for the owner

1. **v1 or v2?** Under v1's scope rule ([131](./131-v1.md), decision 12) a new idea goes to v2 unless it is a bug or polish of something in v1. Claude's recommendation: v1, as Bead's polish and the learning layer's (as [141](./141-bead-rides-the-lfos.md) was taken), since it helps people find their way, built after 150.
2. **What Bead does at each Step:** Claude's recommendation: it goes to its section's edge right above the ringed control, by its usual moves (a stroll along its edge, a leap or fall between sections), then looks down at the control and points with its tail, with a little bounce each time the Step changes. A Step done gets a happy hop. It says no words of its own.
3. **The ring stays.** Claude's recommendation: Bead adds to the ring and never replaces it, so a screen reader, reduced motion and Bead switched off lose nothing.
4. **Where Bead cannot go** (a control in the Display panel, under the docked card, or on a phone's hidden tab): Claude's recommendation: it stays where it is and looks towards it, and goes there once the control is in view (the tab switched, the panel opened).
5. **Its funny turns during a Tour or a Tutorial:** Claude's recommendation: paused, so it never pulls the eye away from the Step; it still answers the knobs as it does now, and dances to the Demo as now.
