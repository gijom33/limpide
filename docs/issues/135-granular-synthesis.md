# 135: Granular synthesis

**What to build:** The owner's idea (inbox, 2026-10-31): "I would like to mention granular synthesis as another v2 candidate." A sound built from many short grains of a wave or a sample, played over one another, their position, length, density and pitch set by knobs; a way of making sound that subtractive synthesis does not teach.

**Blocked by:** [131](./131-v1.md) (v1)

**Status:** needs-triage · enhancement. v2, not committed: kept for after v1, and only if the reaction in the synth world calls for it (the owner, 2026-10-31).

## What a grilling would settle, if v2 comes

- What the grains are cut from: Limpide's own Oscillators, a recorded sound, or both.
- Whether it is a source in the Mixer beside the Oscillators, or an instrument of its own beside the subtractive one.
- What the Viewers show of it: each grain's place in the Scope, and a Spectrum that is no longer Harmonics.
- Whether native Web Audio nodes can make it ([ADR-0001](../adr/0001-native-web-audio-nodes-over-custom-dsp.md)), or it needs custom DSP, and what that means for the plugin.
