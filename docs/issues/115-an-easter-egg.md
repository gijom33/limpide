**What to build:** The owner's idea (inbox, 2026-10-30): "Add Easter egg with Claude dancing or singing."

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done (2026-10-30). Playing B, E, A, D by hand, as in a bead of water, brings Bead, a water drop trailing the Limpide logo, at a spot and in a way at random. About 8 seconds, stopped by any key.
3. **Which Claude?** Claude's logo and its pixel mascot belong to Anthropic, and the repository is public under MIT, so their use needs Anthropic's brand terms checked. Claude's recommendation: an original character, its name only ("Claude") in a caption, the same way About credits Claude Code. The owner may prefer to check the terms and use the real mascot.
4. **Hidden from the tips and the README?** Claude's recommendation: yes, mentioned only in the release notes of the version that brings it.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Playing C, A, D, E calls a small mascot that perches on a section's edge and moves with the music until C, A, D, E sends it away.

**Desired behavior:**
- **Calling it:** the Notes C, A, D, E (any octave), played in that order by the player within a few seconds, from the computer keyboard, the on-screen piano or MIDI, bring the mascot in; the same again sends it away. The Demo, the Song and the Hold rhythm never call it.
- **The mascot:** a small drawing (about the size of a knob), similar to Claude's mascot, in the app's style and both themes. It perches on the top edge of the section of the last control turned, the Oscillators at first, and walks along the edges to a new section when that changes; it never covers a control or a value. (Since decisions 8 to 12: a water drop trailing the logo, coming up from behind the Amp and standing on its top edge.)
- **Reacting:** it moves with what is heard: a hop on each Note's start, a sway with the LFOs, its size or bounce with the Amp's level, still when nothing sounds. It reads the sound the Viewers already read; it adds nothing to the audio graph and no sound.
- **Reduced motion:** with reduced motion asked for, it sits still and only changes pose with each Note.
- **Out of the way:** it takes no focus, no Tab stop and no pointer events; a screen reader hears nothing of it. Not remembered across a reload.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] Playing C, A, D, E brings it, and again sends it away; C, A, E, D does nothing; the Demo playing those Notes does nothing
- [x] It reaches the section of the last control turned, on the phone, 2 × 2, laptop and wide layouts, covering no control (the design audit run with it shown). Since decision 10: it stands on the Amp's top edge at all four, covering no control
- [x] With it shown, the Viewers' frame time stays within the draw-cost test's budget
- [x] No sound changes: an offline render with it shown matches one without
- [ ] The owner sees the drawing and its moves before the release

**Out of scope:**
- Any sound of its own
- A mention in the tips or the README

## How it was built

- **Calling it.** `createCallingPhrase` (`src/input/callingPhrase.ts`) hears each Note the player presses and completes on B, E, A, D in order (C, A, D, E until decision 15), any octave, within 4 s from first to last; a Note out of order starts it over. The audio session now tells listeners of each press (`onPress`), which only the computer keyboard, the piano and MIDI make: the Demo, the Song and the Hold rhythm send their Notes to the engine directly, so they never call it. App brings it up on one completion and sends it away on the next; while it leaves the phrase does nothing. It is not remembered.
- **Its look** (`src/ui/Mascot.tsx`). A water drop on a 22 × 13 pixel grid, 2 px a square (44 × 26 px): light blue with a shine and dark eyes (a deeper blue in the light theme); its tail the logo's stroke in pixels, a saw tooth softening into a sine, in the logo's four colours from green at the drop to blue at the tip. Poses: perched, two walking steps, hop and loud (a splash of droplets over its head), looking left and right, eyes shut, and melting (a squashed landing with droplets flying wide, a puddle with closed eyes, a fading film).
- **Where it lives** (`src/ui/Mascot.tsx`, `src/ui/mascotPlace.ts`). In three and four across, on the top right section's top edge (the rightmost of the top row); its room along the edge is the stretch of spots where, standing, it covers least of the controls and Viewers above (its leaps may brush past the header's buttons; it takes no pointer): the whole edge there, the left end on a phone and in 2 × 2. It comes at a spot at random in its room, one of three ways at random: up from behind its edge over 0.4 s, popping up in half that and hopping, or falling from the top of the window. On a phone and in 2 × 2 (the stylesheet's 860 px breakpoint), `choosePerch` keeps it on a right-column section's top edge while the drop fits between that edge and the header (and the section strip), else takes the edge in view with room for a leap nearest to it, else hangs it under the header, swinging gently from its tip and strolling along it. Sent away on its edge it melts; hanging or in the air it drips away down the window. With reduced motion it is on its line at once.
- **Its moves** (`src/ui/mascotMotion.ts`, pure, its randomness injectable). Its own life, whatever is played: every 1 to 3.5 s a stroll to a spot at random (walking at 28 to 55 px/s, hopping along at 50, or scooting at 115, leaning in), a glance, or, with nothing heard or turned for 3 s, a funny turn: shaking itself like a wet dog (a shiver, droplets flying, its tail whipping), turning round, three bounces, a jelly jiggle, a tail wag, peeking over the edge, a yawn. Blinks every 2 to 6 s. With nothing heard or turned for 20 s it dozes where it is, breathing, and wakes every 8 to 15 s for 6 to 12 s, yawning. With the sound: a hop of 6 px over 0.18 s from each Note's start, a lean of up to 7° with the LFOs that have some Depth, a stretch of up to 10 % with the Amp's level, a splash when loud. With the controls (`gesturesOf` in `src/ui/mascotGestures.ts`, from every Patch change by hand, a Controller, the Demo or a Tutorial): decision 13's moves, each lasting 0.45 s; the same one again within that carries on, so a Cutoff swept every frame walks it along. Leaving: a 14 px jump, a squashed landing with a splash, a puddle, a film fading, 1.2 s in all. With reduced motion it never moves and changes its eyes only on a Note or a control turned. It reads the Modulation model, which now also gives when the newest Note started (`struck`).
- **Between lines, by the laws of motion** (`createMascotPlace`). Gravity is 2,200 px/s². Down to a lower line it hops off its edge at 180 px/s (or lets go of the header) and falls, its speed across set each frame to come down over a spot in the new line; it stretches like a raindrop the faster it falls (up to 35 %). Up to a higher one its tail, turned under it and spinning, pushes at three times gravity until it has just the speed to coast 8 px over the edge, then it swings its tail back, coasts and drops onto the edge from above; to the header it climbs and holds on. Landing, it squashes by as hard as it hit (up to 40 % at 1,400 px/s), splashing wide for a hard landing, and wobbles back like jelly over 0.7 s.
- **Out of the way.** `aria-hidden`, no pointer events, no Tab stop, under the header and the cards. A glossary entry, Mascot; nothing in the tips or the README.
- **Tests.** Unit: the phrase (6); the moves (17: the sound's, strolling within the room with pauses, blinking, strolling even when nothing is heard, funny turns only when nothing is played, dozing in place and waking now and then, the Cutoff walking it both ways and stopping at the room's ends, a sweep turned every frame, the other gestures, waking for a control, the melt in order, reduced motion); each control's move (5); its line and its moves between lines (13: keeping its edge until the drop no longer fits, hanging when none is in view, the nearest edge, following a scroll, a hop off then a fall whose speed grows by gravity each frame and a landing as hard as the fall, a soft short drop, falling across to its spot, a tail push that stops once it can coast just over the edge, climbing to the header and letting go, arriving from the top of the window, turning back mid-fall, reduced motion). Engine: `struck`, and an offline render reading the model every frame matches one that does not. Playwright (`e2e/mascot.spec.ts`, both browsers): calling and sending away, B, E, D, A, the piano in other octaves, the Demo not calling it; it comes into view from behind the Amp or from the top of the window and leaves with a jump before it melts, at once with reduced motion; at 1440 px on the Amp and at 1920 on LFO & Envelopes, once landed, it stays on the edge within the section while strolling, the drop covering nothing; on a phone and in 2 × 2 scrolled down and up it is in view each time it settles, on the right column's edges; in a short phone window it hangs under the header; and the page's frame work with it shown is within 1 ms of without.
