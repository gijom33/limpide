# 131: v1, the web app complete

**What to build:** Nothing of its own: the milestone. The owner framed v1's scope in a grilling on 2026-10-31 ("I would like to frame the scope of the v1 of the Limpide Webapp … That way we will know what needs to be done"), so that the features stop and the polish starts. v1 is a 1.x version the owner deems complete; its frame is in the PRD's [v1 section](../prd.md#v1-the-scope). This issue tracks the work, and every v2 issue is blocked by it.

**Blocked by:** the issues ticked below

**Status:** ready-for-human · enhancement. Done when every box is ticked and the last review round finds only nitpicks; the owner says when.

## Decisions

Decided by the owner, 2026-10-31:

1. **What v1 is:** a 1.x version the owner deems complete, not a 2.0. Once the scope is framed, no new features: only polish.
2. **What Limpide is:** a sound generator only, no effects, no arpeggiator, mono output; with a learning layer (the Tutorials, the Tour, the Demo, the Maths Layer, the overlays) for people getting to know the domain; and every feature the VST/AU plugin will need.
3. **Bead** is the last feature that is not essential.
4. **Ready for the plugin means its features,** not its engine: the engine stays on native Web Audio nodes ([ADR-0001](../adr/0001-native-web-audio-nodes-over-custom-dsp.md)). The port to another language, with libraries to explore, is the plugin project's own work, held to v1's tests and the Ideal Reference.
5. **What a plugin synth needs, in v1:** tempo sync for the LFOs ([112](./112-tempo-midi-clock-and-an-arpeggiator.md), the tempo only), Legato ([119](./119-legato-mode.md)), and channel aftertouch ([132](./132-aftertouch.md)). Out: stereo and MPE.
6. **The other open issues:** in, as polish, [116](./116-patches-at-the-edges.md), [129](./129-words-fun-to-read.md) and [130](./130-the-tour-freezes-to-show-adsr.md); in, as part of the learning layer, [123](./123-a-patch-in-plain-words.md). v2: [120](./120-recording.md), [121](./121-additive-oscillator.md), [122](./122-match-the-sound.md).
7. **The Maths Layer made more valuable and complete** ([133](./133-the-maths-layer-complete.md)): every Section and Viewer writes the formula the engine computes.
8. **The tooltips gain History** ([134](./134-history-in-the-tooltips.md)): with the Maths Layer on, each tip also says where its control comes from, every fact sourced and fact-checked.
9. **Aftertouch:** channel aftertouch only, as a second mod wheel with one Destination chosen in the MIDI panel, the Cutoff by default, its move shown on the Destination's ring.
10. **Done means polished too:** the in issues and the polish list below done, then one last full review round, every profile, the newcomer (profile 16) included, finding only nitpicks.
11. **v1 shows it is done** in its release-log line and the README ("v1 complete"), without a special version number.
12. **After the frame,** a new idea goes to v2 unless it is a bug or polish of something in v1. v2 is a list, not a commitment: "I am not that ready for a v2. It will only depend on what the reaction is in the synth world." Granular synthesis is one such idea ([135](./135-granular-synthesis.md)).
13. **After v1,** the word is spread on forums and Reddit: Claude drafts, the owner posts.
14. **A v2 issue** is blocked by this one and says "v2, not committed" in its Status, so no session or night shift builds it.
