### 248: a crossfade on a change of Filter Mode (full)

**What it does:** each Voice's Filter now has two sides, each with its own stages, Resonance curve and 231 make-up.
- A change of Mode goes to the silent side, and the Voice fades over to it in 10 ms, linearly.
- When no Mode is changing, only the heard side is connected, wired as before.
- After a fade, the old side is disconnected and gets fresh stages.

**Measured** (A3, 1 kHz Cutoff): every pair of Modes, at 0, 50, 80 and 100 % Resonance and both Slopes, now steps at most 1.04 × the wave's own steepest step. Before the fade it was up to 4.1 ×. The offline render of 8 Voices costs the same as before.

**Try:** on Init, hold a Note at 80 % and at 100 % Resonance.
- Switch band-pass ↔ low-pass, and every other pair, at 12 and 24 dB.
- Do the same with an 8-Voice chord held.
- Click through Presets and the picker fast with a Note held.
- A Slope change should still act at once.

**Open to the owner:**
1. The fade is 10 ms and linear. 5 and 12 ms measure the same, and 15 ms removes even the 1.04.
2. The no-click test keeps 231's margin of 1.1. The worst case, 1.04, is the low-pass at 100 % Resonance and 12 dB ringing up after the fade has ended.
3. A Mode change that arrives during a fade waits for it to end, and only the last one asked for plays.
4. The cost was checked with an offline render, not `npm run stats`. That acceptance box is left unticked for the release.
5. "The Presets' levels are unchanged" is ticked because the settled wiring is as before. It was not measured Preset by Preset.
