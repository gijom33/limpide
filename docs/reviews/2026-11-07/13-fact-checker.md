# Review 13: the fact-checker

Checked on 1.26.2, at the owner's asking, in full rather than one release's changes: the words of all 141 Presets (`src/patch/presets.ts`), every tip in `src/ui/tips.ts` (the Viewers', the overlays' and the Timeline's key among them), the Synth Geek Layer's formulas "Under the hood" (`src/music/geek.ts`) and the 34 History lines (`src/ui/history.ts`). Each claim was read against the engine, the Modulation laws and the code. Where a claim is about sound, it was rendered in Chromium from a temporary engine test (`npx vitest run` on a `zzReview13` browser test, since deleted). The test read offline renders with the safety limiter made straight, and read the model through `modulationAt`. The existing claim tests in `src/patch/presets.browser.test.ts` (the "words say", issue 125, Tutorial and issue 137 blocks) were run too, and all 45 passed: they cover Zap Pluck, Kalimba, FM Bell, FM Sidebands, Marimba, Organ, Trill, Talking Filter, Square Lead, the ten Presets of issue 125 and the synced ones. Each History line was read against its sources' notes and grades in `docs/research/134-history-in-the-tooltips.md`. Today's known lines (issues 252 to 263: Felt Piano's Velocity, Vowel's and the swinging Presets' levels, Detune's beats readout, Key Track's and FM's Geek readouts, the C16's words) were left out. Claims that my past reports found true were re-checked quickly where their Preset or tip has changed since (git log). Unless a line says otherwise, a Note is C4 at Velocity 1, on the Preset as shipped.

## False claims

1. **Slow Beats**: "Two sines 4 cents apart: they drift in and out of step, so the Scope's height swells and fades a few times a second." 4 cents is Δf = f·(2^(4/1200) − 1) = 0.0023 f, which is 0.60 Hz on C4. Rendered, the level's dips fall at 0.88 and 2.53 s on C4, so one swell takes 1.65 s. They are 0.98 s apart on A4 and 0.82 s on C5, and each swing is 35 to 45 dB deep. "A few times a second" needs about E6. Wording that holds: "so the Scope's height swells and fades, about once every second or two in the middle of the keyboard, faster higher up."
2. **Rolling Fifth**: "Osc 2 rolls an octave either side of Osc 1". Its Depth is 0.15, and Osc 2 Pitch's law is 12 semitones at full Depth, so the swing is ±1.8 semitones around the fifth. Rendered with Osc 1 silenced, Osc 2 runs from 5.20 to 8.80 semitones above the Note, from just over a fourth to just under a minor sixth. Wording that holds: "Osc 2 rolls about two semitones either side of its fifth, so intervals drift past, from a fourth to a minor sixth".
3. **Square on Square**: "every Harmonic of the lower one present". Counted from the Sub (C3), rungs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 11 sound, but rungs 4, 8 and 12 are at −120 to −131 dB. Neither square has an even Harmonic there: the Sub's odd rungs and Osc 1's 2, 6 and 10 leave every fourth rung empty. Wording that holds: "every Harmonic of the lower one present but every fourth, which neither square has".
4. **The Pulse Width tip**: "How much of each cycle the pulse spends high." The engine's pulse is low for that share, not high. Thin Pulse (10 %) rendered on C3 is above 0 for 89.7 % of each cycle, peaking at +0.04 and dipping to −0.235. A 25 % pulse is above 0 for 75.0 %, and a 75 % pulse for 25.0 %. The Pulse's Geek formula already says so: "the one played is low for d of each cycle" (fixed for 1.26.0). `CONTEXT.md`'s Pulse Width ("the fraction of a pulse Waveform's cycle spent high") says the same as the tip. The Spectrum is the same either way, and Thin Pulse's "a narrow blip in the Scope" holds (a dip). Wording that holds: "How much of each cycle the pulse spends low: at 10 % it is a narrow dip. 50 % is a square." Another fix is to flip the engine's pulse so that the tip and the glossary are right, and the Geek formula then changes.
5. **The LFO plot's tip** (`lfoWaveform`): "Saw ramps up and drops, Saw Down drops and ramps down." Saw Down is the Saw turned over (`lfoShape`: −saw), so it ramps down and then jumps up. It never drops at once. Wording that holds: "Saw Down jumps up and ramps down."

## Loose claims

1. **Init**: "one saw through an open low-pass Filter". Init's Cutoff is 2 kHz, with Resonance 0.2. Against the same saw with the Filter at 20 kHz, on C4, the 4th and 8th Harmonics are +1.6 and +1.9 dB (the Resonance's bump), the 12th is −5.7 dB and the 16th −11.8 dB. It is open for the low Harmonics only. Wording that holds: "one saw through a low-pass Filter set at 2 kHz".
2. **Deep Sweep**: "the Response Curve crosses the whole Spectrum". Read from the model over a cycle, the Cutoff runs from 200 to 3200 Hz, two octaves either side of 800. That is 4 of the Log axis's 10 octaves (20 Hz to 20 kHz, the default), or 4 % to 64 % of the Lin axis. Wording that holds: "the Response Curve sweeps four octaves, from 200 Hz to 3.2 kHz".
3. **Perfect Fifth**: "rolling once every two or three seconds". Osc 2 is 2^(7/12) = 1.49831, so the two-cycle pattern rolls once every 590.7 / f seconds. That is 3.0 s on G3, 2.26 s on C4 and 2.0 s on D4. Higher up it is faster: 1.34 s on A4 and 1.13 s on C5. Wording that holds: "rolling once every two seconds or so around C4, faster higher up". The rest of the line holds ("2 cents short", and Major Third's "14 cents wide" is 13.7).
4. **Self-Oscillation**: "the Cutoff itself starts to sing, a near-sine riding on the saw". The browser's Filter does not oscillate by itself. With Osc 1 down, the Preset is silent (−∞ dB). The song is a Harmonic: on C4 (Cutoff 900 Hz under full Key Track), the 3rd Harmonic (785 Hz) stands at +8.5 dB over the 1st, 2nd and 4th, which sit within 1 dB of each other, while 900 Hz itself, between Harmonics, is at −111 dB. The same holds on C3. "A near-sine riding on the saw" holds. Wording that holds: "the Harmonic nearest the Cutoff rings out 8 dB over the rest, a near-sine riding on the saw". Kick's "sings on Noise" holds, since Noise has energy at the Cutoff itself.
5. **Clang Alarm and Trill** count by cycles where their words count hops. Clang Alarm: "hops between a pure sine and a full metal clang two and a half times a second". It hops 5 times a second, making 2.5 full cycles; my 1.16.0 report gave this as unverifiable. Trill: "it jumps a semitone either way … six times a second". The square at 6 Hz jumps 12 times a second (its test shows a new pitch every twelfth of a second). Wording that holds: "two and a half times a second there and back" and "there and back six times a second".
6. **Rave Stab**: "one finger plays a fifth, a chord a stab of six pitches". A triad gives three Notes, their fifths (Osc 2, 6 cents sharp) and the Sub an octave under each. On C–E–G that is C3, E3, G3, C4, E4, two G4s 6 cents apart, B4 and D5: eight pitches, nine tones. Wording that holds: "a triad a stab of its Notes, their fifths and the Sub under them".
7. **Ride**: "the recipe of an 808's cymbal". No source for it is in the repository. The TR-808's cymbal, as its circuit is usually described, is six square-wave oscillators at clashing pitches through band-pass and high-pass filters, with no ring modulator and no noise. The Preset's parts (two squares through Ring, Noise, a high-pass at 5 kHz) are as described, and "fading over a second and a half" holds (its test). Wording that holds: "squares at clashing pitches through a high-pass, after the 808's cymbal", or a source found and cited.
8. **The Hold tip**: "the held Notes repeat on that rhythm, the last tap starting it over". The last tap does not start it over. After the last tap the loop waits about two beats (`rhythm()`: the last tap's beat, then a beat of rest, rounded to the next beat), then starts again from the first tap. Wording that holds: "the held Notes repeat on that rhythm, with a beat's rest after the last tap before it starts over".
9. **The LFO Destination tip**, Resonance: "across its range (a vocal, whistling sweep)". Only when the knob sits in the middle: the law keeps both LFOs within min(Resonance, 1 − Resonance) each way. Breathing Pad (0.3) swings from 0 to 0.6, not across. The FM entry in the same tip says this ("only as far each way as the FM knob leaves room"). Wording that holds: "across its range, as far each way as its knob leaves room".

Listening judgements, not measured, with their mechanisms checked below: "rubbery", "squelchy", "laser zap", "like a robot mumbling", "like light through glass", "the moving bass of a Juno", "a funky, percussive key", "two tones that do not quite agree" (Cowbell's squares a fifth apart share no Harmonic), Breathing Pad's "never quite the same twice" (0.17 and 0.11 Hz meet again every 100 s) and Choir's "slow vibrato" (5 Hz).

## Checked and true

- **Presets** (the remaining 130 or so): the Shapes' series, gaps and levels (Thin Pulse's tenth Harmonic, and quieter than a square; PWM Morph from 14 to 86 %; Sub Ladder; Sub Two Down; Deep Breath's floor). Octave Stack's 2nd Harmonic is level with the 1st, against a saw's −6 dB. Major Third rolls faster (100.8 / f s against 590.7 / f). Twelfth Bell has three peaks a cycle at any phase. Ring Bell and Ring Octave. Resonant Ring's ripple is at its Cutoff: on C3, Harmonics 10 and 11 (1308 and 1439 Hz) stand level with the 1st, over a dip of −10 dB at the 5th. Reverse Filter's Cutoff is 572 Hz at 3 ms, then 1634 Hz, 3055 Hz and 3857 Hz at 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 s, against its knob's 4000. Kick goes from 440 Hz to 57 Hz in 80 ms (three octaves). Tom is 120 against 55 Hz (an octave and a little), with half the drop. Sidebands on C4 has ±20 Hz sidebands at −7.8 dB and ±40 Hz at −22 dB, apart on the Spectrum's 8192-point FFT (bins under 6 Hz). Key Sync's falling saw starts at its top (`LFO_START`). Ramp climbs for a second. Siren goes an octave each way. Duo Lead's third Note takes the oldest Voice (`synthEngine.ts`). The Velocity claims measured: Pluck Bass hard against soft (0.35) is 3.6 dB brighter at 0.15 s on C2 and 16 dB on C3. Brass Section's "further for a harder hit" holds: its Cutoff is 4224 against 2692 Hz at 0.4 s, and on C4 the 12th, 16th and 24th Harmonics are 6, 14 and 17 dB louder hard. Electric Piano is 8.5 dB brighter hard and Tine Piano 8.6 dB ("a hard one barks"). Marimba's tick holds: the square's own Harmonics (12f and 20f) fall 11 dB in 50 ms, leaving its line at −17 dB, a second sine. Everything my 10-30, 11-01 and 11-02 reports found true still stands where the Preset is unchanged. The changed ones (Kalimba, FM Bell, Tine Piano, Zap Pluck, Talking Filter, Riser) now pass their claim tests.
- **Tips**: the keyboard map (A to ; and W E T Y U O P, Z and X; lower on a key plays harder, Velocity 0.35 to 1). Glow: 15 frames, half every 4.3 frames (71 ms). Lin is 0 to 5 kHz. The Rate runs from 0.05 to 20 Hz. Velocity reaches two octaves, and Key Track counts from C4. The bend's default is 2, and the mod wheel moves LFO 1's Depth. 141 Presets, so Bank Select is needed past the 128th. The Demo's parts are in the order named. The Song is in C minor on the 13 keys. The Destinations' reaches match `LFO_LAWS`, the Resonance's room aside (above). Aftertouch's Resonance goes half way to the top (issue 219). Osc 2's greying matches `osc2Heard`. The Ideal's four marks are a circle, a square, a triangle and a diamond. Glide, Legato, Retrigger, Sync, Tempo and Tap hold, as before.
- **Under the hood**: the Waveforms' series and the Pulse's (aₙ = 4 sin(π n d) / (π n), zero where n d is whole, low for d, as rendered). The Response formula's digital figures: a Web Audio low-pass at 2 kHz reads −44.8 dB at 16 kHz, and two in a row −89.5, against −36.1 and −72.2 analogue, as written. FM's and Ring's rules, the Rates' periods, the note values' conversions, the Envelopes' τ, Glide and the Noise slopes hold as before.
- **History**: all 34 lines match their sources' notes, each resting on an A or B fact. Since my last report, the LFO Rate line now gives the smoother vibrato to the slow sine, as the manual does (the 11-02 item), and FM's line cites S33 and S34 besides. One nuance, not a finding: the research notes Moog's 1964 oscillator as following "a control current", while the Oscillators line says its pitch is set "by a voltage". That is the paper's own title, so the line holds.

Counts: 5 false (3 Presets, 2 tips), 9 loose (7 on Presets, 2 on tips), about 130 Presets' words, about 90 tips and 34 History lines checked true.

## For the inbox

1. Slow Beats says its swell comes "a few times a second", but 4 cents beat once every 1.65 s on C4 (0.98 s on A4), so it should say about once every second or two (review 13).
2. Rolling Fifth says Osc 2 "rolls an octave either side of Osc 1", but its Depth of 0.15 swings it ±1.8 semitones, from 5.2 to 8.8 semitones above the Note (review 13).
3. Square on Square says "every Harmonic of the lower one present", but every fourth rung (4, 8, 12) is missing, at −120 dB, since neither square has it (review 13).
4. The Pulse Width tip and the glossary say the width is the share of the cycle spent high, but the engine's pulse is low for it (a 10 % pulse is above 0 for 90 % of the cycle), as the Pulse's Geek formula says (review 13).
5. The LFO plot's tip says "Saw Down drops and ramps down", but a falling saw jumps up and ramps down (review 13).
6. Init's "open low-pass Filter" is at 2 kHz, taking C4's 16th Harmonic 12 dB down, and Deep Sweep's Curve "crosses the whole Spectrum" over 200 Hz to 3.2 kHz, 4 of its 10 octaves (review 13).
7. Perfect Fifth's "rolling once every two or three seconds" holds only from about G3 to D4: the roll takes 590.7 / f seconds, so 1.1 s on C5 (review 13).
8. Self-Oscillation says "the Cutoff itself starts to sing", but the Filter sings only on a Harmonic: the one nearest the Cutoff is boosted 8.5 dB, nothing sounds at the Cutoff between Harmonics, and with no input the Preset is silent (review 13).
9. Clang Alarm's "two and a half times a second" and Trill's "six times a second" count cycles where the words count hops (5 and 12 a second), and Rave Stab's "six pitches" are eight with its Sub (review 13).
10. Ride's "the recipe of an 808's cymbal" has no source in the repository, and the 808's cymbal is usually described as six squares through filters, with no Ring or Noise (review 13).
11. The Hold tip says the last tap starts the rhythm over, but after the last tap the loop rests about a beat before it starts again (review 13).
12. The LFO Destination tip says the Resonance swings "across its range", but like FM only as far each way as its knob leaves room (Breathing Pad from 0 to 0.6) (review 13).
