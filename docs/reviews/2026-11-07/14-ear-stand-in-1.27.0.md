# Review 14: the ear stand-in, on 1.27.0

Rendered offline in Chromium through `createSynthEngine` (OfflineAudioContext, 48 kHz, velocity 1), in Vitest's browser mode with the engine project's set-up, the safety limiter's curve swapped for a straight line, as on [1.26.2](./14-ear-stand-in.md). The scope: the 25 Presets whose settings changed in `v1.26.2...main` (issues 258 and 267; 266 changed only words), found by comparing every Preset's settings with 1.26.2's, and, since 266 turned the pulse over, the seven Presets that play a pulse beside another Oscillator or the Sub (no factory Preset sends a pulse through FM or Ring): PWM Bass, Clav, Piano, Honky-Tonk, Shimmer Pad, PWM Strings, Strings. 31 Presets in all (Clav is in both lists). The measures are the same as last time: each on C2, C4 and C6, held for its Attack and 1.2 s, released, and rendered through its Release and half a second more (at most 6.5 s); a three-Note chord (C4 E4 G4, 10 ms apart) or a legato change C4 to G4; every semitone from C2 to C7, held for the Attack and 0.6 s for its level and for the Attack and 4 s for its peak, plus a four-Note chord held 4 s on the Presets with Voices; fold-back at 192 kHz on C6 and C7, Noise off, for the Presets with FM or Ring. Three more checks: the Presets with a slow LFO (under 1 Hz) held through one whole cycle of it; Piano and Honky-Tonk rendered Oscillator by Oscillator, so the 1.26.2 sign could be put back by summing them the other way round (the sum of the two renders matches the whole Preset to the last bit, so the renders are exact); and Trance Gate and Ring Lead, 267's engine fixes, as context. The render file was temporary and is deleted.

How to read the table: as on 1.26.2. **C4→C6** is new, beside **C2→C6**. **1.26.2** gives last time's C2 / C4 / C6. **vs group** compares C4 with the median of the rest of its group, where every Preset was rendered the same way. **Worst click** is measured over the 10 ms after a start, release or legato change (25 ms after a chord's start; the limiter's oversampling delays the output by about 4 ms). Fold-back "< −120" means nothing measurable lies past 24 kHz. No render holds a NaN or an Infinity. Every render was silent (−80 dB or lower) by its end, except Dark Drone (−58 dB): its 4 s Release runs past the 6.5 s cap.

## Findings

1. **Piano and Honky-Tonk got quieter with the pulse turned over. Nobody chose this.** Each plays a 35 % pulse with a saw a few cents off, and both Oscillators start their cycles together (issue 94). Before 266, the saw inside the pulse added to Osc 2's saw. Now it subtracts: Piano's sum was 1.5 saw(t) − saw(t−d) and is now saw(t−d) − 0.5 saw(t). The detune turns that around only slowly (the fundamental's beat takes 1.65 s on C4 at 4 cents), and both Presets have died away by then, so their strike now falls in the cancelling half of the beat:
   - Piano: −29.4 / −29.2 / −27.1 dBFS on C2 / C4 / C6, against −24.4 / −24.5 / −25.1 with the 1.26.2 sign (−24.4 / −24.5 / −25.1 measured last time, so the emulation is exact). That is 5.0 dB quieter on C2 and 4.7 dB on C4, 0.2 dB on C7. Its peak halves (0.08 against 0.15), and its chord is 4.5 dB down (−24.3 against −19.8).
   - Honky-Tonk: −32.2 / −28.2 / −27.0 against −24.9 / −25.4 / −27.7: 7.3 dB down on C2 and 2.8 on C4. Its swing across the keyboard turns from −2.8 dB into +5.2 dB.
   - 266's call 1 expects that "detuned … nothing changes on average". That holds for a held sound's long-run mean, not for a decaying strike. Both Presets still pass the group test (Piano −1.3 against the rest of the Pianos). But Piano was the Pianos' loudest and now sits 4.7 dB under its 1.26.2 self, and Felt Piano now stands +3.2 dB over the rest of the group by this measure.
   - Suggested setting: no knob puts the old sign back. Pulse Width 0.65 gives Piano back only 0.4 dB on C4 (−28.8) and Honky-Tonk 1.5 dB (−26.7), and Piano's Volume is already at 1. Three ways out, for the owner: (a) take 266's own alternative in the engine (a delay of the rest of the cycle, which keeps the sign) for an Osc 1 pulse heard with Osc 2; (b) rebuild the two Presets around the new sign, for example Osc 1 a saw and Osc 2 the pulse with Osc 2's mix raised, and render them again; (c) keep the new sound, if the owner's ear prefers the softer strike, and hold Piano and Honky-Tonk where they now sit in the level tests (258's "within 1 dB" list does not cover them, so nothing would catch the next change).
2. **Zap Pluck falls across the keyboard, as 267 predicted, and is uneven in its top octave.** C2 −30.7, C4 −29.0, C6 −37.2: −6.5 dB from C2 to C6 and −8.2 dB from C4 to C6. Both are over the 6.5 dB line, so the known point is confirmed. The scan reaches a low of −40.0 dBFS on E6, 11.3 dB under its loudest semitone (F#4, −28.7). The top octave goes jagged: E6 and F6 −40.0 and −39.9, F#6 −36.2 (a 3.7 dB step between neighbours), A6 −34.5, A#6 −36.9. The bite is gone: 1.0 % of its energy lies above 5 kHz on C6 (5.6 % in the worst 85 ms window; 72 % on 1.26.2), and its fold-back on C6 and C7 is < −120 dB (−79 / −78 before). The level and the bite are the same energy. Key Track 0.3 keeps C6 at −33.9 but brings back 18 % above 5 kHz (46 % worst window). Key Track 0.5: −30.9 and 64 %. Cutoff 2.8 kHz: −36.5 and 2.3 %. Cutoff 3.5 kHz with Key Track 0.3: −30.1 and 71 %, the old bite. Suggested setting: none of these does both. Either the owner accepts the fall in exchange for the bite (267's call 3; its steady sine after the strike is as loud as before), or the strike is made less bright at its source, for example a lower FM (crossMod 0.35 to 0.4) with the Filter back near 4 to 5 kHz, which this review did not render. Whichever the owner picks, a level test should hold Zap Pluck's C6 and C7, which nothing holds now.
3. **Vowel is at the 6 dB line on this measure.** C2 −21.1, C4 −18.7, C6 −15.1: +6.0 dB from C2 to C6 (issue 258 measured −21.6 / −19.5 / −19.0). The difference is its 0.4 Hz LFO: a 1.2 s hold reaches further up its swing than 258's 1 s. Held through a whole LFO cycle (2.5 s), its loudest 100 ms is −19.6 / −17.6 / −14.4 (+5.2 dB) and its mean −22.1 / −20.2 / −19.7 (+2.4). C5 and C7 play at −14.2 and −14.3, 3.4 dB over C4, as 258's call 6 expected. Within the bound, but loudness grows steadily up the keyboard. Suggested setting, if the owner hears it: Key Track 0.6 (not rendered here), or leave it as it is.
4. **Clang swings 6.4 dB from C2 to C4, the most of the changed Drums.** C2 −26.6, C4 −20.2, C6 −21.2 (C2→C6 +5.4, as on 1.26.2). Over the scan, from −26.6 (C2) to −19.4 (G#6). Its Volume went up, but its high-pass at 600 Hz is fixed, so a low Note loses its Ring product. Under 258's C2-to-C6 bound, but past it from C2 to C4. Suggested setting: Key Track 0.5 on its high-pass, as Cowbell got (not rendered).
5. **The Kick and the Tom now start with a sharper strike.** They bend 0.008 and 0.006 in the first 10 ms, ×46 and ×22 their steady ring, the same on every Note (no Key Track), where 1.26.2's bent under 0.001. That is their Pink Noise into a resonant Filter at 1 ms Attack, at 10 dB more level. It is in the range of Pick Pluck's pick (0.008), a drum's strike, not a fault. Their DC rose to −58.5 and −56.9 dBFS (−82 and −79), the low weight of Pink Noise over a decaying hit: inaudible. Nothing to change.
6. **Levels beside their groups.** On C4, every changed Preset sits within 3 dB of the rest of its group, but Felt Piano (+3.2, finding 1) and Clav (−2.9, as on 1.26.2). The Drums now sit by the Basses: Kick and Tom −17.6, the kit from −22.3 (Snare) to −17.6. The Kick is 1.9 dB under 808 Boom (−15.7). Within the slow-LFO check, Deep Sweep held through a whole 5 s cycle peaks at −10.9 on C4, 6 dB over its 1.2 s figure: its LFO, not a change of this release.
7. **What 258 and 267 fixed holds.** Every one of 258's fourteen keyboard Presets keeps its C2→C6 within 6 dB (Vowel at 6.0, the rest 5.2 or less). The three top-octave Pads hold their C7 within 6 dB of their C4 in the scan: Sweep Pad −21.4 (C4 −23.2), Breathing Pad −22.9 (−23.7), Dark Drone −18.9 (−22.4). Resonance Swell peaks at 0.38 on every semitone, its neighbours within 0.1 dB. Trance Gate's joining Notes bend 0.007 to 0.010, ×0.17 to ×0.25 the gate's own switches, for Notes 5 to 100 ms apart (×3 to ×7 on 1.26.2). Ring Lead's fold-back measures −114 / −113 / −110 dB on B5 / C6 / C#6 and −114 on C7 (−57 on C6 before).
8. **Brightness, for the record.** Ride (99 %), Open Hat (97 %) and Snare (23 %, C2) are Noise by design. High-pass Buzz has 14 % above 5 kHz on C6, Resonance Swell 19 % on C6, Deep Sweep 9.7 %.
