These are the `c16Layout` and `c16Card` tips in `src/ui/tips.ts`; the printable card's heading, instructions, footer and cells (`src/ui/C16Card.tsx`, from `src/midi/c16.ts`); About's "Support" line and the README's new line; the MIDI panel's mode labels (`src/midi/describe.ts`) and its "C16 only" note (`src/ui/MidiPanel.tsx`); and the 1.19.0 line of `docs/issues/25-releases.md`, against issues 145, 143, 140, 142, 146, 138, 131 and 112 and ADR-0017. The code read: `src/midi/c16.ts`, `src/midi/controlSurface.ts`, `src/midi/encoderMode.ts`, `src/midi/inputs.ts`, `src/midi/mappings.ts`, `src/ui/hooks/useMidi.ts`, `src/ui/App.tsx` and `src/ui/LfoControls.tsx` (where each control sits on screen), `src/ui/mascotPlace.ts` and `e2e/mascot.spec.ts`. ## False or loose claims

1. **Release log:** "its knobs set to Relative #1 move a control 1 % a step at any speed". This is false. A 65 moves 1 %, but a 66 moves a third of that (`C16_BURST_STEP = 1 / 3` in `relativeSteps`), and past a speed one bit of turning is a burst of eight 66s, 2.67 %. The test "counts a fast turn's 66 as a third of a step" shows it: 23 slow messages and 11 fast bits (88 66s) give 0.23 + 0.88 / 3 = 52 %, where 1 % a bit would give 34 %. Issue 145's last "Since then" line has it right: "a C16 knob's 65 moves 1 %, a 66 a third of that". The release line repeats decision 13's undone wording.
2. **Release log:** "The C16 plays over Bluetooth MIDI too (143)." This is true of the device, but the release did nothing to make it so, and the line reads as new. `isC16` is the same regex as in 1.18.0 (`/tempopad\s*c16/i`), which already matched "Synido TempoPAD C16 BT Bluetooth"; only its comment changed. The check was made on a Mac (PRD Further Notes, issue 34), not on other systems. Issue 143 is still `needs-triage`, and its three questions are unanswered.
3. **`c16Layout` tip:** "each strip a column of the section: the top knobs its top row, the faders its levels or Envelope". This holds for the Amp and the LFOs but is loose for banks A and B. Bank A's K1 to K4 (Osc 1 Octave, FM, Ring, Pulse Width) are not a row on screen. Pulse Width is in Osc 1's first row and Octave and FM in its second (`App.tsx`), while Ring is the Mixer tab's third fader, so a strip spans three tabs. The Mixer has five faders, and Ring's level is on a knob. Bank B's K1 to K4 are the Cutoff row, which is the section's second row, under Mode and Slope. Sub Octave, a switch, is on S4, not an R button. The release log's "a column of its Section on screen" has the same looseness, and on bank D one LFO spans two strips.
4. **`c16Layout` tip, `how`:** "Applied by itself the first time a C16 is plugged in". The layout is applied by itself only when no Mappings are stored (`fresh` in `useMidi.ts`: `mappingStore.list().length === 0 && C16_APPLIED === null`). A person who already has their own Mappings gets nothing applied, apart from the upgrade of issue 34's layout.
5. **Release log:** "A Buy Me a Coffee link in About and the README (146). Its frame-cost test runs in Chromium only". Here "Its" follows the coffee link, so it reads as the link's test. The test is Bead's (`e2e/mascot.spec.ts`, issue 138).
6. **MIDI panel, the "Momentary" label:** MIDI Learn makes every Action Mapping `trigger` whatever the button sends (`learnFrom`), so a toggle button learned on Hold reads "Momentary" and fires on every other press only. This behaviour is old (the label used to read "Press"), but the new word now names how the button sends, and for a toggle button that is wrong. It is minor.

## Unverifiable from the code

- **The card:** "Choose a bank with CTRL BANK, then A to D", "The pads play the synth" (true in KEYBOARD mode, PRD story 51; the card does not name the mode) and "Free places stay Toggle, the C16's own setting". These are the device's own behaviour, known from the owner's C16 and photo (issue 145, review 17), not from the code.

## Checked true

- **`c16Layout` tip:** the bank order (A Oscillators, B Filter, C Amp, D LFOs); "M1 to M4 are the Preset arrows, Freeze and Hold, and S3 Snapshot" (`SHARED_M`, `shared`); "It answers the C16 only, so another keyboard's pedal and mod wheel keep theirs". On that last point, `handle` skips a `device: 'c16'` Mapping unless `fromC16`, which comes from the input's name, and the message then reaches the SUSTAIN and MOD_WHEEL defaults (test at `controlSurface.test.ts:414`). Also "replacing your Mappings": `restore` replaces the whole list.
- **`c16Card` tip:** it opens a page (`#c16-card` in `main.tsx`, a new tab); it is one page (`@page c16-card`, `e2e/c16Card.spec.ts` for A4 and Letter); and it shows every bank's knobs, faders and buttons, each button marked Momentary or Toggle (48 marked).
- **The card:** its cells come from `C16_BANKS` and `knobCc`, `faderCc` and `buttonCc`. These match issue 145's CCs (knobs 1 + 8b, faders 33 + 4b, buttons 49 + 12b down each strip; bank B's M2, Next Preset, is CC 64). The footer's M1 to M4 and S3 match `SHARED_M`. "All on MIDI channel 1" holds: `CHANNEL = 1`, and the parser's channels count from 1. The modes come from `buttonMode`: 22 momentary buttons (5 a bank, plus bank A's two Waveforms), and the two-way switches toggles.
- **Release log:** each of the four strips is two knobs, a fader and M, S and R. The Preset arrows, Freeze, Hold and Snapshot are on every bank. The buttons are momentary or toggle by what they do. The layout answers a C16 alone. The card opens from the MIDI panel while a C16 is connected (ADR-0017 exists and says so). Bead lands where its leap shows (140: the sign of `-vy` in its time to landing, for its own jumps, the owner's case). It stands on the line under a phone's section tabs (142: `overlap`, while the edge shows by 2 px or more). The frame-cost test runs in Chromium only, as WebKit's clock counts whole milliseconds (138; Playwright has only Chromium and WebKit). v1's scope was framed (131), and the tempo and Sync were grilled (112). The code review's fixes and inbox lines are in (`3ed0fbf`, `137feba`).
- **About and README:** "Support: Enjoying Limpide? Buy me a coffee", and the README's "☕ Enjoying it? Buy me a coffee.", both link to https://buymeacoffee.com/gijom33, the address in issue 146.
- **MIDI panel:** `trigger` reads "Momentary" and `toggle` reads "Toggle" (`describe.ts`), and "C16 only" shows on exactly the Mappings with `device === 'c16'`.

Counts: 6 false or loose (1 false, 5 loose), 3 unverifiable, about 30 claims checked true.

## For the inbox

1. The 1.19.0 release notes say a C16 knob moves "1 % a step at any speed", but a fast turn's 66 moves a third of 1 %, so one fast bit moves about 2.7 %.
2. MIDI Learn makes every Action Mapping momentary, so a toggle button learned on an Action fires on every other press, and the panel now labels it "Momentary".
