## Findings

1. **The 141 ride test still says Bead follows the plot a frame behind.** `e2e/mascot.spec.ts:1169-1170`: "It follows the plot a frame behind, so the slower the frames the further it trails: within 2 px at about fifteen a second in WebKit on Linux, 4.4 on GitHub's slower runner (1.22.0's branch run)." Issue 270 removed that lag. Bead used to read the line under where it stood the frame before. Now it reads the line under its spot this frame, for its foot and its lean (`onItsLine(moving)` then `lineUnder(pose.x)`, `src/ui/beadFrame.ts:613-615`; `acrossOf(where, pose.x)`, `:685`). The plot's box is now the drawing inside its border (`src/ui/beadPage.ts:136-140`). 270 measured about half a pixel off on average in both browsers (`e2e/mascot.spec.ts:1259-1266`). Its unit tests in Bead's frame dropped the same "a frame behind" (`src/ui/beadFrame.test.ts:183`). The 6 px bound can stay, but its reason has changed. Wording that holds: "On the line under its spot each frame (issue 270): a few pixels allowed where the clock Bead reads and its plot's differ within a frame, on a slow runner."

2. **"The board along the line" in `docs/bead.md`, now that Bead rides lines steeper than its board.** `docs/bead.md:98`, the Demo's surf: "the board along the line and Bead leaning with it". The same paragraph says rides outside the Demo use "the same board". The lean stops at 35° (`STEEPEST_SURF`, `src/ui/mascotPlace.ts:574`). At 2.5 Hz, half Depth, three across, the line is up to about 70° steep (issue 270, "How it was built" 2 and 3, and its call 2). So for most of each rise and fall the board is at 35° and not along the line. Before 270, the 1 Hz bound kept rides on gentler lines. The comment at `src/ui/beadFrame.ts:682` ("on its board along the line under it") says the same. `surfLean`'s docstring (`mascotPlace.ts:576-580`) does give the 35° cap. Wording that holds: "the board along the line, as steep as 35°, and Bead leaning with it".
