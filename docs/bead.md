# Bead's secrets

Everything Bead, Limpide's hidden water drop, can do. Bead is an Easter egg: the tips and the README leave it out on purpose ([115](./issues/115-an-easter-egg.md), decision 7), so this page is the one place it is all written down. How each thing is built is in [115](./issues/115-an-easter-egg.md) and [127](./issues/127-more-tricks-for-bead.md).

Bead makes no sound and changes nothing you hear. Turning a control only changes how Bead looks or moves.

## Calling it, and sending it away

- **Play B, E, A, D**, as in a bead of water, within 4 seconds, in any octave: on the computer keyboard (J, D, H, S), the on-screen piano or a MIDI keyboard. Bead comes, in one of three ways chosen at random: up from behind its edge, falling from the top of the window like rain, or popping up with a hop.
- **Play B, E, A, D again**: it leaps, smashes itself down into a splash, becomes a puddle, then a film that fades. Hanging or in the air, it drips away down the window instead.
- The Demo, the Song and Hold's rhythm never call it: only Notes you play yourself.
- Once you have met it, **Display has a Bead switch**, remembered. Off, Bead melts away and the Notes do nothing; on brings it back.
- Bead is not remembered across a reload.

## Where it lives

- **Three or four sections across**: on the top edge of the top right section (the Amp, or LFO & Envelopes), along the stretch where it covers least. Every 15 to 40 seconds it visits another section in view for a while: falling to one below, or leaping in an arc to one beside it.
- **On a phone and in 2 × 2**: it stays in view as you scroll, on the top edge of a right-hand section. To go down it falls like rain and splashes; to go up it turns its tail down under itself and flies on it. With no edge in view it hangs under the header, swinging from its tip.

## Its own life

- It strolls (walking, hopping along or scooting), glances about and blinks.
- With music it **dances**: a step at each Note, a hop as each Note starts, a lean with the LFOs, a stretch with the Amp's level, a splash when loud.
- At an end of its edge it sometimes looks over, takes fright and scoots back.
- After 20 seconds with nothing heard or turned it **dozes**, breathing, waking now and then with a yawn.

### Funny turns

When nothing has been played for a few seconds, it does a funny turn now and then, dealt like a shuffled deck so none comes twice before all have come. The four jokes come three times as often.

- **The jokes:** drinking a glass of water poured into its mouth, then a burp; a rain cloud and a big umbrella; a water gun, fired with recoil, then a blow on the barrel; a fountain show, two jets swinging and crossing, then a finale straight up.
- **Water gags:** evaporating in the sun and being rained back by a cloud; a soap bubble it gets caught in, floating up until it pops; skipping rope with its own tail; fishing off its edge: it casts, the hook drops down past the bottom of the window, bobs while it waits, jerks at a bite, and comes back up on the line with a little ♪, which Bead shows off.
- **The others:** a wet-dog shake; a happy waddle dribbling water; jumping in a puddle that is not there; a sneeze; a moonwalk; chasing its tail; a dizzy spin; a little fountain; hiccups; a twist; balancing on one foot; three bounces; a turn round; a jelly jiggle; a tail wag; a peek; a yawn.
- **Changing the Filter Slope** while nothing has been played cues the next funny turn.
- **Once begun, a funny turn plays to its end**: a Note, a control turned or a visit to another section waits for it. A tickle, a click, picking it up or a word still interrupts it.

## What the controls make of it

Every knob and fader you turn, a Controller turns or the Demo turns gets an answer from Bead:

- **Cutoff, Filter Envelope Amount, Key Track, Velocity:** it walks right as they open, left as they close.
- **The Cutoff at its lowest:** Bead freezes into an ice cube, still and shivering. Open the Filter and it melts, dripping.
- **Octaves, Semitone, Osc 2's Envelope Amount:** a hop up for higher, a crouch for lower.
- **Pulse Width and the Envelope faders:** it stretches taller or squashes down.
- **Resonance:** it shivers. Turned past three quarters, it screams for a moment: eyes wide, tail straight.
- **FM, Ring, Noise:** a shiver. **Volume and the Mixer's levels:** a splash going up, a wince going down. **The LFOs' Rate and Depth:** a wiggle. **Glide:** it slides along without stepping.
- **A Waveform chosen** (Osc 1's or Osc 2's): its tail becomes that wave for 3 seconds (sine, triangle, saw, square or pulse), then the logo again.
- **Detune**, while both Oscillators are in the Mixer: Bead splits in two. The more cents, the farther apart the two drops, and the faster they wobble, as two detuned Oscillators beat. At 0 they are one again.
- **The Noise heard:** pixels of static crackle around it, more as the Noise's fader goes up.
- **Any other choice** (a Mode, a Destination): a glance. **A Preset loaded:** an excited hop.

## Words spelled with Notes

While Bead is out, five more words spelled with Notes, like B, E, A, D, each cue a trick. Each word must be played within 4 seconds, in any octave. One Note can end one word and start the next.

| Notes | On the computer keyboard | Bead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D, E, A, D | S, D, H, S | plays dead: keels over onto its back, crosses for eyes, peeks to see if anyone noticed, pops up |
| F, A, D, E | F, H, S, D | fades to a ghost, floating, and back |
| C, A, F, E | A, H, F, D | a tiny cup of coffee, two sips, then wide awake: it strolls faster for a while |
| D, E, A, F | S, D, H, F | covers its ears |
| B, A, G, G, A, G, E | J, H, G, G, H, G, D | drags a suitcase along its edge |

## Playing with it

- **A mouse over it tickles it:** it squirms and giggles.
- **A click or a touch** sends tiny droplets every way.
- **Press it and move**: you pick it up, dangling and wriggling. Let go and it is thrown at the speed you moved, under gravity, bouncing off the sides of the window. It lands with a splash on the first section edge it comes down onto, and stays there a while. Let go under its edge, it flies back up on its tail.
- **Too much** tickling, clicking or throwing (about 3 seconds of tickles, or five quick clicks) and it runs off, eyes shut, and keeps away from the pointer a few seconds.
- **Its eyes follow the mouse** while the pointer is near it.

## The theme and the date

- **Switching the theme** in Display: for the dark theme it puts on a nightcap and yawns; for the light one it squints and shades its eyes. It reacts once Display is closed, so you can see it.
- **On your date:** a ghost sheet on 31 October, a Christmas hat from 24 to 26 December, and a party hat on 24 October, the day Limpide went on GitHub.

## During the Demo

Through the Demo's parts, in turn, Bead:
- conducts with a tiny baton, down on each Note;
- plays air guitar on its tail, strummed on each Note;
- surfs an LFO's shape: it jumps onto an LFO's plot and rides the line, end to end, as the LFO moves it. Where no LFO plot is in view, it conducts or plays air guitar instead.

## With reduced motion

With reduced motion asked for by the system, Bead comes and goes at once and never moves. It changes only its pose (its eyes on each Note or control turned, its tail, the ice, its twin, its costume). It cannot be picked up.
