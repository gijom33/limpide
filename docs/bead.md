# Bead's secrets

Everything Bead, Limpide's water drop, can do. Bead lives on the page from a first visit, and the README and Display's tip say so ([150](./issues/150-bead-shown-by-default.md)); its tricks are left out of them on purpose, to stay a surprise ([115](./issues/115-an-easter-egg.md), decision 7), so this page is the one place they are all written down. About shows a few: Bead stands beside its logo, and a click on it gives a card of its tricks that links here ([163](./issues/163-bead-in-about.md)). How each thing is built is in [115](./issues/115-an-easter-egg.md), [127](./issues/127-more-tricks-for-bead.md) and [150](./issues/150-bead-shown-by-default.md).

Bead makes no sound and changes nothing you hear. Turning a control only changes how Bead looks or moves.

## Calling it, and sending it away

- **It comes by itself**, while Display's Bead switch is on, 3 seconds after the page opens, in one of three ways chosen at random: up from behind its edge, falling from the top of the window like rain, or popping up with a hop. On a first visit it waits until the welcome is closed, and whenever About is open.
- **Play B, E, A, D**, as in a bead of water, within 4 seconds, in any octave: on the computer keyboard (J, D, H, S), the on-screen piano or a MIDI keyboard. It goes away for this visit: it leaps, smashes itself down into a splash, becomes a puddle, then a film that fades. Hovering or in the air, it drips away down the window instead.
- **Play B, E, A, D again**, and it comes back. While it melts, the Notes do nothing. A reload goes back to what Display's switch says: on, Bead comes by itself again.
- The Demo, the Song and Hold's rhythm never call it or send it away: only Notes you play yourself.
- **In About**, while Display's switch is on, Bead stands to the right of the name "Limpide" at its size on the page, its foot on the name's baseline, the logo and the name centred as without it ([178](./issues/178-bead-in-about-at-its-own-size.md)), blinking, glancing about and wagging its tail, now and then jiggling or hopping (still, with reduced motion). A click, Enter or a tap on it gives its card in place of the credits: who it is, what it answers, a few funny turns, how to send it away, and a link to this page. Back, Escape or Bead again return to the credits. With the switch off, About has no Bead ([163](./issues/163-bead-in-about.md)).
- **Display has a Bead switch**, from the first visit, on by default: the lasting setting, remembered across visits. Off, Bead melts away and never appears again, by itself or by the Notes, which then do nothing, until the switch is turned on; on brings it back. The Notes never turn the switch.

## Where it lives

- **Three or four sections across**: on the top edge of the top right section (the Amp, or LFO & Envelopes), along the stretch where it covers least. Every 15 to 40 seconds it visits another section in view for a while: falling to one below, or leaping in an arc to one beside it.
- **It rides the LFOs** ([141](./issues/141-bead-rides-the-lfos.md), [197](./issues/197-bead-rides-everywhere.md)): while an LFO is on (its Depth above 0), a Sine or a Triangle at 2.5 Hz or slower ([270](./issues/270-bead-rides-up-to-2-5-hz.md); with Sync, the Hz its note value gives), its plot in view and clear of the cards, Bead rides its line, as in the Demo's surf, for 8 to 18 seconds, then goes back to its edge. Three or four across, a ride comes first whenever a visit is due; on a phone and in 2 × 2, one comes every 15 to 40 seconds, Bead falling onto the plot like rain or flying up to it on its tail, and going back the same way. **Dropped near the plot** of an LFO it may ride (its box over the plot or within its own width of it), it lands on the line and rides it for 8 to 18 seconds; near a Saw's, a Square's, a fast one's or one at Depth 0, it lands as anywhere. The plot's own tip, opened by a pointer resting on it, does not keep it off the line. It goes back sooner when the LFO stops being one to ride or its plot goes out of view or under a card (on a phone or in 2 × 2, its plot gone under the tabs, it goes to an edge in view, or hovers, as when its edge goes under them); picked up, it lets go of the line; sent away, it jumps home and melts there. Never while the Demo plays or with reduced motion, a drop included.
- **On a phone and in 2 × 2**: it stays in view as you scroll, on the top edge of a right-hand section. To go down it falls like rain and splashes; to go up it turns its tail down under itself and flies on it. With no edge in view, as when its edge goes under the tabs, it takes off on its whirling tail and hovers where it lost ground, a little under the tabs or the header, bobbing gently, until an edge comes into view to drop or fly onto ([142](./issues/142-bead-on-the-line-under-the-tabs.md)). With reduced motion it hovers still.
- **Never under a card** ([164](./issues/164-bead-never-under-a-card.md)): it never stands where the Demo's card, a Tutorial's, the Tour's, a tip or an open panel covers it, and strolls only along what they leave clear. When one comes over its spot it goes to the nearest clear stretch of an edge in view; with every edge in view covered, it stands on the card's top edge until one is clear again: the topmost card's, when two are docked. Its whole drawing counts, its Detune twin and its tail turned round included. With nowhere clear at all, not even its hover line on a phone (under a page-wide description), it goes out of sight, up under the tabs or down behind its edge, and comes back once somewhere is clear ([192](./issues/192-beads-cover-checks-complete.md)). It is still drawn under them, so it never covers their words or buttons.
- **Never over text where it rests** ([181](./issues/181-bead-never-over-text.md)): wherever it stands still, on an edge, on a phone's tab line or on the card's top, its drop, its tail (swung down too, as when pointing the way) and its Detune twin cover no label, readout, heading or tab name; it strolls only along the stretches clear of them, a few pixels apart. On its way, hovering under the tabs (the page moving under it), surfing or riding, it may pass over text.

## Its own life

- It strolls (walking, hopping along or scooting), glances about and blinks.
- **It faces its way** ([179](./issues/179-beads-tail-trails-behind.md)): strolling, riding or leaping to the right it turns round quickly, its tail trailing on its left; to the left, and standing still, its tail is on its right, as in the logo. A step shorter than itself, a dance step to a Note or a funny turn does not turn it; hovering, it faces as still.
- With music it **dances**: a step at each Note, a hop as each Note starts, a lean with the LFOs, a stretch with the Amp's level, a splash when loud.
- At an end of its edge it sometimes looks over, takes fright and scoots back.
- After 20 seconds with nothing heard or turned it **dozes**, breathing, waking now and then with a yawn.

### Funny turns

When nothing has been played for a few seconds, it does a funny turn now and then, dealt like a shuffled deck so none comes twice before all have come. The four jokes come three times as often.

- **The jokes:** drinking a glass of water poured into its mouth, then a burp; a rain cloud and a big umbrella; a water gun, fired with recoil, then a blow on the barrel; a fountain show, two jets swinging and crossing, then a finale straight up.
- **Water gags:** evaporating in the sun and being rained back by a cloud; a soap bubble it gets caught in, floating up until it pops; skipping rope with its own tail; fishing off its edge: it casts, the hook drops down past the bottom of the window, bobs while it waits, jerks at a bite, and comes back up on the line with a little ♪, which Bead shows off.
- **The others:** a wet-dog shake; a happy waddle dribbling water; jumping in a puddle that is not there; a sneeze; a moonwalk; chasing its tail; a dizzy spin; a little fountain; hiccups; a twist; balancing on one foot; three bounces; a turn round; a jelly jiggle; a tail wag; a peek; a yawn.
- **Changing the Filter Slope** while nothing has been played cues the next funny turn.
- **Once begun, a funny turn plays to its end**: fishing goes on until Bead gets its ♪. A Note, a control turned, a visit to another section, dozing off or freezing waits for it; a tickle or a click is not felt; a word or a theme switch comes once it ends. Only picking Bead up or sending it away cuts it short.

## What the controls make of it

Every knob and fader you turn, a Controller turns, the Demo or the Tour turns gets an answer from Bead:

- **Cutoff, Filter Envelope Amount, Key Track, Velocity:** it walks right as they open, left as they close.
- **The Cutoff at its lowest:** Bead freezes into an ice cube, still and shivering. Open the Filter and it melts, dripping.
- **Octaves, Semitone, Osc 2's Envelope Amount:** a hop up for higher, a crouch for lower.
- **Pulse Width and the Envelope faders:** it stretches taller or squashes down.
- **Resonance:** it shivers. Turned past three quarters, it screams for a moment: eyes wide, tail straight.
- **FM, Ring, Noise:** a shiver. **Volume and the Mixer's levels:** a splash going up, a wince going down. **The LFOs' Rate and Depth:** a wiggle. **Glide:** it slides along without stepping.
- **A Waveform chosen** (Osc 1's or Osc 2's): its tail becomes that wave for 3 seconds (sine, triangle, saw, square or pulse), then the logo again.
- **Detune**, while both Oscillators are in the Mixer: Bead splits in two, its twin to its left below 0 cents and to its right above, whichever way Bead faces ([174](./issues/174-beads-detune-twin-on-both-sides.md)). The more cents, the farther apart the two drops, and the faster they wobble, as two detuned Oscillators beat. At 0 they are one again.
- **The Noise heard:** pixels of static crackle around it, more as the Noise's fader goes up.
- **Any other choice** (a Mode, a Destination): a glance. **A Preset loaded:** an excited hop.

## Words spelled with Notes

While Bead is out, five more words spelled with Notes, like B, E, A, D, each cue a trick. Each word must be played within a second a Note, from its first Note to its last: 4 seconds for the four-Note words, 7 for BAGGAGE ([204](./issues/204-baggage-air-guitar-and-surfing-seen.md)), in any octave. One Note can end one word and start the next.

| Notes | On the computer keyboard | Bead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D, E, A, D | S, D, H, S | plays dead: keels over onto its back, crosses for eyes, peeks to see if anyone noticed, pops up |
| F, A, D, E | F, H, S, D | fades to a ghost, floating, and back |
| C, A, F, E | A, H, F, D | a tiny cup of coffee, two sips, then wide awake: it strolls faster for a while |
| D, E, A, F | S, D, H, F | covers its ears |
| B, A, G, G, A, G, E | J, H, G, G, H, G, D | drags a suitcase along its edge |

## Playing with it

- **A mouse over it tickles it:** it squirms and giggles.
- **A click or a touch** sends tiny droplets every way.
- **Press it and move**: you pick it up, dangling and wriggling. Let go and it is thrown at the speed you moved, under gravity, bouncing off the sides of the window. It lands with a splash on the first section edge it comes down onto, and stays there a while. Let go under its edge, it flies back up on its tail.
- **Too much** tickling, clicking or throwing (about 3 seconds of tickles, or five quick clicks) and it runs off, eyes shut, and keeps away from the pointer a few seconds.
- **Its eyes follow the mouse** while the pointer is near it.

## The theme and the date

- **Switching the theme** in Display: for the dark theme it puts on a nightcap and yawns; for the light one it squints and shades its eyes. It reacts once Display is closed, so you can see it.
- **On your date:** a ghost sheet on 31 October, a Christmas hat from 24 to 26 December, and a party hat on 24 October, the day Limpide went on GitHub.

## In a Tutorial and the Tour

While a Tutorial or the Tour is open, Bead points the way ([151](./issues/151-bead-points-in-the-tour-and-tutorials.md)), adding to the ring round the control, never in its place:
- **It goes to the control**: in a Tutorial, the Step's (the Display button while a Step's option is in its shut panel); in the Tour, the first control each card turns, or the one it names (the Display button, the Tutorial button, the Song button). It makes its usual move to that control's Section, a leap or a fall to its top edge, then slides down the panel like a drop on glass and clings at the control's side, level with it. To another control of the same Section it slides across. It stays with the control as the page scrolls.
- **Where it clings**: on the side where it covers least of the neighbouring controls and names, the side away from the card when both are as free, never over the ring or the target written above the control. Where it would cover a label beside the control (Osc 1's "Waveform", the Cutoff's name), it clings a little lower, just under the label; with no room below, a little farther along; of two sides as free, the one where it need not move ([181](./issues/181-bead-never-over-text.md)).
- **It points**: it faces the control, looks at it and aims its tail at it, with one small bounce on arriving and at each new Step or card; otherwise it stays still but for blinking. The pointer passes through it there, to whatever is under it.
- **Out of reach** (in the open Display panel, under the card, on a hidden tab, off the window), it stays where it is and looks towards it: beside the last control while that is still in reach and it need not turn round to look, else on its edge (issue 210). It goes once the control is shown.
- **A Step made**: a happy hop. **A Tutorial finished**: a little fountain.
- **Meanwhile** its funny turns, its visits and rides, its dancing and dozing pause. It still answers the controls, the words and the theme.
- **Closed**, it hops back up to its edge and its own life comes back.

## During the Demo

Each Demo part takes the next of three acts after the last part's ([204](./issues/204-baggage-air-guitar-and-surfing-seen.md)), a Demo begun starting with the first:
- conducts with a tiny baton, down on each Note;
- plays air guitar: its tail straight, raised on its usual side like a guitar's neck, shaken on each Note; eyes shut, rocking, a little hop on the loud Notes;
- surfs an LFO's shape: it jumps onto the plot of an LFO that is on (its Depth above 0) and rides the line on a surfboard, end to end, as the LFO moves it, the board along the line, as steep as 35°, and Bead leaning with it, forward going down the wave and back going up, its tail out behind. With no LFO on, or none in view and clear of the cards ([164](./issues/164-bead-never-under-a-card.md): from three across the Demo's own card opens small in the bottom right corner, clear of the plots ([230](./issues/230-the-demos-card-small-in-the-corner.md)), but moved over one it keeps Bead off it), it conducts instead, and the next part plays air guitar. Played from part 1, it surfs first at part 5, then at 8 and 13, where a plot is clear. Outside the Demo it rides a slow Sine or Triangle now and then (above), on the same board.

## With reduced motion

With reduced motion asked for by the system, Bead comes and goes at once and never moves. It changes only its pose (its eyes on each Note or control turned, its tail, the ice, its twin, its costume). It cannot be picked up. In a Tutorial or the Tour it stays on its edge and turns its eyes and tail towards the control, without the hops.
