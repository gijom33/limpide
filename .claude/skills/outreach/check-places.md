# Checking the places

For each place in `docs/outreach/places.md` (or the one place named):

1. **Find its rules at the source:** a subreddit's rules (`https://www.reddit.com/r/<name>/about/rules.json`, its sidebar and wiki), a forum's rules or FAQ page and the description of the board you would post in, a site's "submit a tip" page. Read them, not a summary of them.
2. **Fill the place's Rules cell** with what bears on a developer posting their own free tool:
   - self-promotion: allowed, where (its own post, a weekly thread, a board for announcements, a flair) and how often;
   - links: allowed in the post or only in a comment;
   - AI: any rule on AI-made posts or AI-built software;
   - the account: minimum age or karma;

   each with the link it came from and the date checked. A rule you cannot find: "not found: ask the moderators". Done when every row has each of the four, or says where it was not found.
3. **Set the Cadence cell** to the stricter of the place's own rule and the plan's default (`plan.md`, Cadence).
4. **Suggest up to three places** that are not on the list, under the table, each with who is there and why Limpide fits.

Leave `places.md` changed and uncommitted, and say in the report which rows changed.
