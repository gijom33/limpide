# Checking the places

For each place in `docs/outreach/places.md` (or the one place named):

1. **Find its rules at the source:** a subreddit's rules (`https://www.reddit.com/r/<name>/about/rules`, its sidebar and wiki), a forum's rules or FAQ page and the description of the board you would post in, a site's "submit a tip" page. Read them, not a summary of them. A site that refuses automated reading: use what third-party summaries say, mark it **unverified**, and name the pages for the owner to read.
2. **Fill the place's fields** with what bears on a developer posting their own free tool, each with the link it came from:
   - **How to post:** self-promotion allowed or not, where (its own post, a weekly thread, an announcements board, a flair, a day) and how often;
   - **Links:** in the post or only in a comment, limits for new accounts;
   - **AI:** any rule on AI-written posts or AI-built software; where AI-written text is barred, end the field with **Notes, not prose.**;
   - **Account:** minimum age, karma, trust level or status.

   A rule you cannot find: "not found". Done when every place has the four, or says where they were not found, and the date at the top says when.
3. **Set the Cadence** to the stricter of the place's own rule and the plan's default (`plan.md`, Cadence).
4. **Suggest up to three places** under "Places to look at next", each with who is there and why Limpide fits; a place found active and fitting goes into its section.

Leave `places.md` changed and uncommitted, and say in the report which places changed.
